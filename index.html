Quest to one billion

Billion Beers

Photograph the beer. Rate it out of ten. Watch the number climb.

Billion Beers is a social app for logging the beers you drink. Every beer you log is photographed, rated and given its own permanent number — its place in a global count heading toward one billion.

See what friends are drinking, watch beers being logged around the world as they happen, collect the countries you have drunk in, and form a team to take on other teams at weekends.

Currently in testing

The app is in private beta on TestFlight. If you would like to try it, get in touch.
